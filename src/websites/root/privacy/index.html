Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 7, 2026

This policy details how your data is managed across our different services: the website (wixonic.fr), the KCMaths² web application, and the WixiBot Discord Application.

1. Contact

  • Contact: contact@wixonic.fr
  • Discord Contact and Support: go.wixonic.fr/help

2. Data Collected by Service

A. Website (wixonic.fr) & KCMaths²

We use local storage mechanisms and cookies according to your choices:

  • LocalStorage: solely to remember your cookie consent.
  • Analytics Cookies (Optional): Firebase Analytics (Google).
  • Server Logs: Temporary technical logs, deleted on restart.

B. KCMaths² (Specific)

Within the context of the school year, we collect:

  • Identity: Names/Usernames.
  • Activity: Virtual currency, game statistics.
  • Retention: Deleted in Summer 2026.

C. WixiBot

Data collected for feature operation.

  • Stored Data: Discord ID, activity stats, roles.
  • Storage: JSON files on private infrastructure.
  • Giveaways & Tickets: Archived for traceability.

3. Retention Period and Deletion

Service Retention Period Deletion
Server Logs Ephemeral On restart
KCMaths² School Year Summer 2026
WixiBot Indefinite On request

Right to be Forgotten

To request manual deletion for WixiBot:

  1. Leave the relevant Discord server.
  2. Email contact@wixonic.fr.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell any data. Third-party services:

  • Google (Firebase): Hosting & Analytics.
  • Discord: Subject to Discord's Terms.

5. Your Rights

In accordance with GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, and delete your data options. Contact us via email.

Note: Non-European users can also benefit from these rights.
