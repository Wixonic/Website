From Third Parties

  • Other Users and the Public: Depending on your account settings, we may share personal data with other users and the public. You control what information is made public.

C. Data Retention

We retain personal data as long as your account is active. This data is necessary for the operation of our products and websites. When you delete your account, all personal data associated with you will be permanently erased from our systems.

D. Your Privacy Rights

You have specific legal rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: We provide you with all personal data we hold upon request.
  • Right to Rectification: We help you correct your personal data without undue delay.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Upon request, we delete all personal data without undue delay.

If you wish to exercise your rights, please contact us.

This section addresses specific use cases where certain features or services involve unique handling of your personal data.

1. W.L.M.A. (Discord Application)

Our Discord application is available on GitHub. You are free to audit the code as you wish.

When you interact with our Discord Application, the following types of data may be collected:

  • Account Data: Your Discord ID, username, and display name. Your Discord email is collected only if you log in or sign up via Discord on our website.
